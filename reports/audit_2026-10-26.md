# DeltaRSI cached-data stability and code-integrity audit — 2026-10-26

**Decision: no new strategy approved.** The exercise found reproducible research/alert defects; they were corrected and CI regression tests added. No real orders were submitted.

## Scope and methodology

- Source: existing repository CSV files only, **no exchange candle downloads**: ETH 8,760 1H, BTC 8,761 1H, ICP 8,761 1H, HYPE 5,003 1H; plus cached 4H volume (2,190 bars for ETH/BTC/ICP and 1,252 for HYPE).
- Time coverage: 2025-10-26 through 2026-10-26 for ETH/BTC/ICP; 2026-04-01 through 2026-10-26 for HYPE.
- Independent JS reimplementation of SMA21 → Wilder RSI12 → EMA7 → symmetric logarithmic DeltaRSI, ATR14, 4H turn signal. The fixed baseline is **2.5 ATR stop / 3 ATR target / 12-bar time exit**, entry at next 4H open. Stops win ambiguous same-bar hits; adverse stop gaps are filled at the worse of bar open and stop.
- Per asset, five sequential non-overlapping 20%-of-history evaluation slices, with indicator warm-up from earlier candles but no trade returns spanning slice boundaries. Position overlap disallowed. One 4H trade per instrument at a time.
- All returns below are **full-notional trade/equity simulations**, not returns for the proposed 0.25%-of-equity stop-risk position sizing. Trading costs: 0.15% round-trip baseline; 0.25% and 0.40% round-trip stress. Funding, borrowing, impact, and liquidation risk remain unmodeled.
- Important: the dataset and many strategy variants were already inspected in earlier experiments. **These slices are chronological diagnostics, not a new untouched out-of-sample dataset**. No claims of statistical significance or independently verified profitability are made.

## Ablations — not new strategy recommendations

- **Volume above prior 20-bar mean**, full cached sample: ETH 77 trades PF 1.59, BTC 76 PF 1.59, ICP 81 PF 1.43, HYPE 36 PF 1.05. On the final fifth slice: ETH PF 0.98, BTC 3.62, ICP 1.80, HYPE 2.24. The filter's ETH performance deteriorated in later slices; the small HYPE sample is not convincing.
- **Volume below prior 20-bar mean**, full sample: ETH PF 1.10, BTC 1.24, ICP 1.41, HYPE 0.93. Direction is not consistently better across assets.
- **Short-only turn** was promising on BTC (70 trades PF 1.85; slice PF 0.59, 2.56, 1.39, 1.79, 3.76) and ICP (72 trades PF 1.71; slice PF 6.35, 2.59, 1.25, 2.21, 0.99), but failed on HYPE (40 trades PF 0.71) and was selected after examining these slices. It is **not independent validation**.
- **Trend-following EMA50** reduced trade count, but was not uniformly superior; existing 3,240-config train/validation/holdout research also shows severe winner's curse, with top training configurations losing on final holdout.

## Defects found and corrected

1. **Completed 4H candle dropped in paper alert engine.** The shared `aggregate()` always removes the final bucket, including when four closed 1H candles exist. Thus `paper_signal_4h.py` could silently miss/delay the newest 4H signal at exactly the time a completed 4H bar is available. Corrected by `aggregate_completed_4h()` with four-hour completeness and UTC close-time checks. Four regression tests cover complete, incomplete, still-open, and next-partial-bucket cases.
2. **Out-of-split execution in `feature_walkforward.sim()`.** The old loop permitted a signal on the last bar of a split, entry at the next split's open, and a forced exit at the previous split's close (exit time earlier than entry). Corrected with `k < hi-1` and `entryi < hi`. This affects only boundary cases, but it is a correctness defect.
3. **Train/test price leakage and mislabeled drawdowns in `research_multiasset.py`.** Training accepted signals immediately before the 70% cutoff and valued them using prices in the test period; held-out simulations were previously generated over the full history and filtered afterward, so training positions could suppress the earliest test entries. The field `test_max_drawdown_by_asset` previously measured full-history DD. Corrected by embargoing the final `hold` bars in training, running the held-out segment independently, and computing test-only DD. For the fixed 4H turn baseline, the regenerated held-out result is 154 trades, PF 1.266; by asset ETH PF 1.625 (43 trades), BTC PF 1.655 (43), ICP PF 1.110 (44), HYPE PF 0.946 (24). Corrected held-out full-notional DD: ETH 15.53%, BTC 10.54%, ICP 22.07%, HYPE 15.52%. These are still historical data already used for strategy development.

## Release gate

No improved system has passed a genuinely untouched forward sample. Keep **PAPER ONLY**, with HYPE research-only. Future research should freeze one rule set, collect new forward candles only as time passes, verify TradingView indicator parity and live paper fills, include funding and mark-price stop execution, and evaluate portfolio drawdown under actual risk-based sizing. Do not promote the volume or short-only variants based on these reused data.
